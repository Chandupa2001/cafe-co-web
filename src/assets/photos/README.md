# Photos

Put photos in this folder using these names. Any common format and size works, for example `hero.jpg`. Each photo replaces its placeholder automatically.

For the minimalist look, similar natural light and a calm, uncluttered background across all the photos will make the biggest difference.

| Name | Where it appears | Shape | Best shot |
|---|---|---|---|
| `hero` | Top of the page | 16:9 on desktop, tall on phones, so keep the subject central | Your best single shot of the cafe or a coffee (already added) |
| `property` | Next to "Pull up a chair" | Tall | The shopfront or the room |
| `detail` | Under "Pull up a chair" | Tall | A close-up: latte art, a pastry, a pour |
| `menu` | Beside the menu | Tall on desktop, wide on phones | Coffee and food on a table |
| `gallery-1` to `gallery-6` | "A peek inside" | Tall | A mix of food, drinks and corners of the cafe |

Alt text for each photo is in `src/data/photos.ts`. Update it if a photo shows something different.
